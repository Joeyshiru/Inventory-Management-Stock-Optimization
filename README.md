# Inventory Management & Stock Optimization | Excel Project

## Project Overview

This project analyzes inventory levels, stock movements, reorder requirements, product performance, and supplier lead times using Microsoft Excel.

The objective is to improve inventory visibility, identify stockout risks, support replenishment decisions, and provide actionable insights through an interactive dashboard.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor current inventory levels and stock availability.
- Identify low-stock and out-of-stock products.
- Calculate reorder quantities to support replenishment planning.
- Analyze inventory turnover and slow-moving products.
- Evaluate stock distribution across product categories.
- Assess supplier lead times to support purchasing decisions.

## Tools & Techniques

**Microsoft Excel**

- Data cleaning and preparation
- Excel Tables and structured references
- SUMIFS, COUNTIF, IF, IFERROR, and XLOOKUP
- Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts
- Inventory turnover calculations
- Conditional formatting
- Interactive slicers
- KPI dashboard development

## Dataset Description

The project uses a simulated inventory dataset representing 50 products, 8 suppliers, and 1,500 stock movement records.

The dataset includes product information, supplier details, opening stock, purchase receipts, sales, customer returns, damaged quantities, reorder levels, and target stock levels.

## Key Findings

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Total Current Stock | 14,352 units |
| Total Current Inventory Value | KES 55.42M |
| Low-Stock Products | 8 |
| Out-of-Stock Products | 2 |
| Average Inventory Turnover | 1.73 |
| Longest Supplier Lead Time | S005 — 15 days |
| Highest Stock Category | Electronics — 3,328 units |

### Inventory Availability

The analysis identified 8 low-stock products and 2 out-of-stock products, representing 20% of the product catalog requiring inventory attention.

### Inventory Turnover

The average inventory turnover was 1.73, providing a baseline for assessing inventory movement and identifying slow-moving products.

### Product Category Performance

Electronics recorded the highest current stock at 3,328 units, accounting for approximately 23.2% of total inventory units.

### Supplier Performance

Supplier S005 recorded the longest lead time at 15 days, highlighting the importance of considering supplier delivery timelines when planning replenishment.

## Business Recommendations

1. **Prioritize stock replenishment:** Address the 2 out-of-stock and 8 low-stock products to reduce potential lost sales and operational disruptions.
2. **Improve reorder planning:** Use calculated reorder quantities and target stock levels to support purchasing decisions.
3. **Monitor slow-moving inventory:** Review products with low inventory turnover to identify potential excess stock and opportunities for improved stock allocation.
4. **Strengthen supplier planning:** Account for supplier S005's 15-day lead time when scheduling purchases and reviewing safety stock requirements.
5. **Optimize category-level inventory:** Monitor Electronics stock against demand trends to maintain appropriate inventory levels.

## Interactive Dashboard

The Excel dashboard provides visibility into inventory performance through five KPI cards and five analytical charts.

It includes a Product Category slicer that dynamically filters inventory metrics and visualizations.
